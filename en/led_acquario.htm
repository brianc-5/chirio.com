AQUARIUM LIGHTING

How to build a ceiling light kit with LED and power supply for 220V "Do it yourself" by R.Chirio

LED & lightingRoberto Chirio

Marine Aquarium with medium dimensions, 250 liters illuminated with 48 LED 3W White+Blue. 

The aluminum ceiling fixture is kept 10cm away from the water surface.

KIT 220V with 9 led P4 star

 Composed of:

  • 9 Led Seoul P4 star White
  • 3 resistors 1.2 ohm 5W
  • 1 power supply 220/12V 3.5A

 orderable directly from:  info@chirio.com

Payment by Postepay or Paypal  

- Fast delivery 3 -

With this kit it is possible to achieve powerful aquarium lighting, composed of 9 P4 star LEDs for a total of over 2160 Lumens. 

For example, by fixing LEDs on a 1m x 40cm aluminum plate and distributing them over three rows, you can create a cover to illuminate an aquarium of medium size, with a total consumption of just 40W from 220V. There is no need to put lenses on the LEDs as the 130° exit angle is suitable for close and diffuse lighting across the entire surface of the aquarium.

Countless other applications of your choice, just use a little imagination.

  • Available on request lenses of 10°, 25° and 45°,. to satisfy any lighting need.
  • Available P4 BLU LED to increase the color temperature to 10000° K or 14000°K

10000°K solution composed of 9 White LEDs 6300°K + 3 Blue LEDs (40W)

14000°K solution composed of 6 White LEDs 6300°K + 3 Blue LEDs (30W)

- Connecting the LED DIMMER RGB it is possible to obtain light fades with sunrise/sunset effect.

  Example of how to fasten and connect the 9 P4 LEDs on an aluminum plate of 20x40cm.

 IMPORTANT the LED is a delicate electronic component, pay close attention to the connection and respect the electrical characteristics. All power LEDs heat up considerably during operation, they must be mounted on a proper heatsink before powering on. 

Never look directly at the light of the LED when on, it can cause eye damage.

Use a soldering iron with low power (max 30W) and solder the terminals before fixing the star LED to the heatsink.

KIT 220V with 48 led P4 star

 Composed of:

  • 39 Led Seoul P4 star White
  • 9 Led Seoul P4 star Blue
  • 13 resistors 1.2 ohm 5W
  • 3 resistors 2.7 ohm 5W
  • 1 power supply 220/12V 16A

 orderable directly from:  info@chirio.com

Payment by Postepay or Paypal  

- Fast delivery 3 -

With this kit it's possible to create powerful lighting for large aquariums, made up of 48 P4 star White and Blue LEDs for a total of over 9000 Lumens, with power consumption of 190W, suitable for aquariums over 300 liters with dimensions from 150 x 60 x 60cm and beyond.

  • All LEDs must be mounted and connected on an aluminum heatsink plate, with the dimensions of the tank, also a cover must be obtained to possibly mount the 12V fans to cool the LEDs.
  • Connecting the LED DIMMER RGB it is possible to obtain light fades with sunrise/sunset effect.

Comments

With LED lighting excellent results can be obtained both in brightness and energy savings. 

By optimizing LED placement and providing good cooling it is conceivable to achieve 50% energy savings compared to neon tubes.

- Connecting the LED DIMMER RGB It's possible to get light fades with dawn/dusk effect, turning on and off with crossfade the white and blue LEDs, and on the third driver channel you can add red LEDs to enhance the dawn-dusk effect. Easily programmable via USB software.

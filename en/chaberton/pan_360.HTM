Esplanade

29/07/2005

600 Kb

Friday 29 July 2005. Waiting for the race to arrive Iron Bike 2005 on the summit of Chaberton and taking advantage of an opening in the clouds it is possible to have a 360° view from the top of the clearing next to the 8 towers.

The image has a size of 6000x848 pix and has been compressed with jpg format.

East side

18/09/2004

580 Kb

This 360° panorama illustrates the entire view we can have by positioning ourselves at the far left of the ramparts, moving the view clockwise, we see below us the line of towers 8, 7, 6, at the back Valle di Susa down to Exilles, followed by the long flat area in front of the towers, at the bottom the final part of the path and Chaberton pass, followed by the fort courtyard with the concrete foundation for the flagpole and the wooden support of the temporary aerial ropeway.

The image has a size of 6000x955 pix and has been compressed with jpg format.

Tower n4

21/08/2004

700 Kb

This 360° panorama illustrates the entire view we can have by climbing on tower no. 4, moving the view clockwise, we see below us the line of towers 5, 6, 7 and 8, at the back Clotesse peak, Valle di Susa down to Exilles, followed by the Assietta ridge, the Sansicario slopes and the work for the bobsled run, below the Oulx-Cesana road and at the back the beginning of the Argentera valley, to the right the Thuras valley, below us towers 3, 2 and 1. To the right follows the long flat area with stone and concrete arches to level the mountain.

The image has a size of 6000x952 pix and has been compressed with jpg format.

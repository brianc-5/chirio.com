The Chaberton Battery

Fortifications

  

A visit inside the Chaberton Battery and the bunkers is at risk of danger, no responsibility is assumed for damage to people and property.

Whoever enters does so at their own risk.

VISIT INSIDE THE CAVERNS AND ARTIFICIAL TUNNELS:

  • Never venture alone inside unknown cave entrances.
  • Always state what the destination is.
  • Bring more than one torch of excellent reliability per person, and know its runtime.
  • In the cave the temperature averages 0 to 10°C, even in summer. (Below zero at Chaberton) Proper clothing is necessary.
  • For safety it is always better to have at least 10mt of rope and possibly wear a protective helmet, in the presence of ice crampons are necessary.
  • Do not rely on your mobile phone, as there is no signal inside the tunnels, you must go outside and move to the Italian side.
  • Do not remove what little remains.
  • Do not leave any traces of your passage.

Photographs and videos inside bunkers and underground tunnels:

 To take good photographs inside the fortifications it is necessary to have a powerful flash (ng 38/45), sometimes it is better to have a second flash to use with a servo cell. So as not to have hard shadows it is good to point the flash upward, where and when the ceiling and walls are of light color.

 A 28mm optics or better 24mm allows the best results of field of view as the rooms are generally very narrow. Ideal is the 17-35mm zoom that covers all the wide-angle needs of the reportage well. With a 28-200mm zoom you can frame and highlight even the details.

 Indoors if the flash is not powerful, it is good to set the sensitivity to at least 400 ASA, you can also get good results without flash, using the camera on a tripod and illuminating the entire room with a good lamp. (Pose B - open-flash)

 Brand-name digital cameras with medium-high characteristics allow you to get good results both outside and inside the bunkers. I recommend setting the sensitivity to minimum values (50/100 ASA) when outside on sunny days. For interiors set the sensitivity to 200/400 ASA values to get the best performance from the built-in flash coupled with good depth of field. Better results if you set the color temperature to flash.

It is better to choose the top models that allow you to use a higher power external flash.

 Video recordings should be made with good lighting, using battery-powered lamps. A 20W illuminator is sufficient for amateur use, but if we want better results I recommend a 100W illuminator. Excellent LED illuminator http://www.chirio.com/led_video_3p7.htm

 Inside the cave fortifications in addition to the low temperature, there is strong humidity and continuous water dripping.

Where humidity is not present it's possible to find very fine dust similar to cement, which is easily raised by walking, pay some attention to interchangeable optics.

 It is advisable to have spare batteries and protect the equipment with appropriate weather/dust protection.

A head lamp to be placed on your head will allow you to move easily and have both hands free.

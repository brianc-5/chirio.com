The Chaberton Battery

A former Italian military fortress at 3130 meters: virtual tour curated by Roberto Chirio.

Roberto Chirio Last update: 20 July 2010

The Chaberton Battery finished being built in 1910 at the top of Monte Chaberton, always represented a threat to France because of its strategically dominant position over the entire valley of Briançon.

Eight large towers built on the mountain top to house eight 149/35 cannons, pointed menacingly toward France, which gave the Chaberton Battery the aura and charm of an impregnable fortress. Located at 3,130 m a.s.l., Europe's highest fortification today attracts many curious and passionate hikers.
Those who travel through Valle di Susa and particularly toward Cesana, Sestriere and Claviere, cannot escape the sight of that majestic construction on the summit of Monte Chaberton.

Many Italian Fortifications like this one became French as a result of the Peace Treaty signed in 1947.

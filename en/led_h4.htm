LED lamp H4

Construction of replacement lamp for H4 halogen "DIY" by R.Chirio

November 2014

At a commercial level, LED lamps capable of replacing the H4 are already available as well as the H3 and H7. With this construction you want to verify the temperature and brightness limits of a retrofit system to be applied to traditional projectors, not designed from the start to adopt LED lamps.

The first advantage in using LEDs is the reduction of power consumption at a level of 4-5 times less than the traditional filament of 55W 12V

If the operating parameters of the LEDs are respected, we will have a lifespan even 10 times greater than a halogen lamp, this means that for the life of the car it will no longer be necessary to replace burned-out/broken lamps.

prototype lamp with 10W LED 1000Lumen

Classic H4 lamp 55W filament

January 2015

H4 lamps with filament get quite hot, they must therefore be mounted inside reflectors that withstand heat of over 100°C at the socket. The reflectors are molded with thermosetting resin and must last a long time with the lamps on.

By mounting an LED as a replacement we will no longer have infrared ray emissions therefore reduced heat.

For good light output it is good to replace old reflectors with yellowed and opaque reflective surfaces, as well as front glasses if opaque should be replaced.

LED group.

  • The H4 LED made at the lathe and mill from an aluminum bar.
  • At the moment only the dipped beam LED is present (low beam)
  • The LED center has been positioned exactly at the center of the filament of the traditional lamp.

H4 group with 12V power supply

H4 group with SW2800 regulator, it is always better to have one regulator for each LED.

It's good to put a 4AT fuse in series with each lamp.

Commercial H4 lamps

Car 120 LED 3528 SMD H4 Xenon-White Fog Head Light Headlight Lamp Bulb DC 12V

  • Neither the power dissipated nor the quantity of emitted light is specified.
  • The 360° distribution of the LEDs and also the positioning along the length leads to multiple focal points that give practically mad light not suitable for circulation.
  • The lack of a heat dissipation system leads to think that the LEDs reach limit temperatures to the detriment of their lifespan.

DO NOT USE!!!!

HONSCO H4 24W 1800lm 5000K L/H Cool White Light LED Car Headlight Kit (DC12~18V / 2 PCS) 

  • 1800 Lumens with both LEDs on thus intended as high beam, with 1 LED only so crossing light we are at 12W 900Lumens
  • Good construction with forced ventilation.
  • Not waterproof, especially the Driver!

- 

You can install it but it's not homologated!

Comments

- It is always better to use approved lamps. Or better yet the entire original optical group.

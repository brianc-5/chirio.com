LED lamps Road test

"DIY" by R.Chirio

LED & lightingRoberto Chirio

March 2007.

After the design and construction of LED lamps the first thing to do is compare "on the road" the optical performance of the realized solution.

The images that follow are the first intended to compare optical performance and light distribution; they were all taken manually with an exposure of 5 sec and an aperture of f. 4.0. The camera positioned at the 0 meter point on the vertical axis with the light source under test.

The study aims to find the best solution for extreme MTB lighting (night races) compromise between optical performance and realization costs.

As you can clearly see, the output of an inexpensive torch with only 51 5mm LEDs is superior to 100 LEDs at 40000mcd with correct and stabilized power. On the other hand in the torch the LEDs are driven beyond their rated specifications and this is not reliable and is only valid as an absolute comparison, but is not usable on the road.

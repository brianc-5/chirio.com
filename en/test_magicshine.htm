Final remarks:

It was the first of the MagicShine models, released 3 years ago, excellent for those who want to make a night excursion with friends, for a 24h it is a bit short, especially in a curve.

Using a second MJ808 lamp mounted on the helmet, excellent results are obtained in racing.

It is the best for competitive use, excellent brightness values on curve. The controls are very functional.

With the convenient button control it's possible to change beam and power with extreme simplicity without taking your hands off the handlebar, by adjusting power long runtimes can be achieved.

It is the most powerful of the series, suitable for those who give maximum output in just 2 hours of strong light, as it is very inconvenient to press the buttons to reduce power. It starts immediately strong with all the light available.

Recommended for those who always go to the max, even at night....

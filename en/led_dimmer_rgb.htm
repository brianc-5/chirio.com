LED DIMMER RGB 3 channels

Brightness control for LED aquarium ceiling lamp Automatic sunrise sunset effect "LED lighting" by Roberto Chirio

2012 / 2013

With this board it is possible to give great realism to the LED lighting of the aquarium.

The board is simplified to the maximum, there are no buttons or display, there's no complicated programming procedure, truly an installation and operation simplified to the maximum.

Just connect LED and timer input, it works right away!

It works well with single LEDs connected in series, or you can connect LED strips or LED bars of the desired color, or a single RGB bar.

The base software allows gradual on and off for sunrise and sunset, using 1, 2 or 3 PWM driver channels.

  • Using an external timer you give command to the input, for when you want to turn on, so after 10 minutes the white led channel will be stable at maximum brightness.
  • With two channels connected therefore blue and white, blue will start first and after 10 minutes white also arrives.
  • With three channels connected and adding a row of three red LEDs, it is also possible to have the pink/red hues typical of tropical sunrises/sunsets.

The LED transition time is easily modifiable via a switch on the board.

LED connection diagram

Practical diagram for the connection of various components.

  • The red cable corresponds to +12V, the black cable to negative. The blue cable to the negative (-) of the LEDs.
  • Respect the polarity in the LED series, to simplify the channels they are represented by a single row, but in the case of 160W there are 13 rows of white LEDs and 3 rows of blue LEDs.
  • LED channel power connections must be made with appropriately sized cables, for 10A use at least 1.5mmq cable, if longer than one meter better to use 2.5mmq.
  • To connect the LEDs just use a small cable of 0,35mmq, preferably with silicone insulation suitable for high temperatures.
  • Pay attention to the connection of the 220V Timer input, use approved plugs and cables.
  • The relay function on the board is to take the timer command and keep the board isolated from the 220V mains.

LED strip connection diagram

Practical circuit for connecting various components to the dimmer board 2013/01

  • The red cable corresponds to +12V, the black cable to negative. 
  • Respect the polarity of the LED strips, to simplify the channels they are represented by a single row, you can also add a row of red LED strips connected to the RED terminal.
  • LED strips should not be longer than 1 meter, if more light is needed, they must be cut and connected in parallel. 
  • All strips must always be glued to an aluminum plate of aquarium cover size.

- With system off position the dip switch to desired time, position (1) corresponds to on so set to ON.

- With position 000, all three switches must be down, the TEST function is activated.

- The Test function progressively turns on and off all 3 channels in a time of 10 seconds, so you can verify the correct operation of all LED channels, without waiting for the long fade times.

- To change programming you must first turn off power, change position on the dip switch and then restore power.

Timing diagram of ignition

  • S0 represents the Timer connected to the relay.
  • With the activation of S0 the fade, increases the PWM command of the blue and red leds, reach the maximum after t0=10 (20, ... , 70)minutes. (see timing table)
  • Subsequently the white LED channel starts, descends and the red turns off.
  • Finished the dawn phase we are at full with white and blue LEDs on.
  • By turning off the S0 input, the sunset phase is activated always of 10 (20, ... 70)minutes.
  • The whites turn off and the reds turn on always with the blues on.
  • Last step gradual shutdown blue and red.
  • The system goes into standby and waits to be reactivated S0

Comments

With LED lighting excellent results can be obtained both in brightness and energy savings. 

This dimmer is very reliable and easy to connect, and being already programmed, does not need buttons and display. 

Suitable therefore for all those who want simple and easy-to-use systems, without complicated buttons and display.

By optimizing the arrangement of LEDs and adding a row of red LEDs it is possible to achieve excellent rendering of sunrises and sunsets.

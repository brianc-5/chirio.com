Chirio Mini Whip SDR+ 5V

Active antenna for frequencies 10 kHz - 30 MHz - how to modify RTL-SDR

Radio & antennasRoberto Chiriorubric "Radio Corner"

July 2018

This MiniWhip is the evolution of the SDR as it eliminates the external battery power and directly uses the 5V available on the input of many SDR receivers.

Power supply reaches the antenna through the signal coaxial cable.

The characteristics are those of the MiniWhip SR for handheld scanners, but in this configuration the antenna can be mounted outside using a 20 mm PVC tube as protection and support.

The tube can be easily positioned on balconies, terraces and also just outside a window. This configuration is very suitable for mounting on campers and why not also outside a tent.

MiniWhip SDR+ 5V PCB version

MiniWhip SDR+ 5V base fixed version (PVC tube 20mm)

Characteristics

  • With 5V the current draw is approximately 10mA. 
  • Bandwidth from 10 kHz to 30 MHz (-30dB), at -80dB reception is still up to 150 MHz
  • Output impedance is 200 ohm suitable for pretty much all receiver inputs.
  • PCB version dimensions 17x180mm suitable for tests
  • Fixed base version 150mm long sealed inside 20mm PVC tube.
  • Available accessories: power feeder 5V battery input 9V, 10mt extension with RG174/U cable SMA-M/SMA-M connectors

Signal present on Chirio Mini-Whip, you can see all frequencies from 0 to 20 MHz. The signal is stronger and many stations come through that are not listenable with a conventional wire antenna. Very strong all signals down to a few tens of kHz. The antenna was mounted outside the house on a 3mt PVC pole.

  • The simple connection between the MiniWhip SDR+ and the input of the SDR receiver, in this case it connects to the HF input, with the RTL.SDR to have 5V on the HF input it is necessary to make the modifications indicated below.
  • In this configuration, no ground connection is needed to remove interference since we do not have a separate power cable.
  • The photo is just demonstrative of the simple connection, the MiniWhip antenna must be mounted externally far from the SDR.

How to modify the cheap RTL-SDR to have power on the HF connector

  • To bring power to the HF input you must open the RTL.SDR case by unscrewing the 4 screws and extracting the board.
  • In the entrance area there is already a hole for testing with the 5V pad

  • You need to solder on the circuit at the indicated point an inductor of 1mH so as to make a bridge for the power supply and block for the RF towards the power supply itself. 
  • You must also cut the trace below to proceed after the SMA HF and insert a 100nF ceramic capacitor, otherwise you find yourself in a DC short circuit condition due to the transformer present further down on the PCB.
  • To activate 5 V just move the jumper to the right in position (A) note that it also brings power to the UV input so remove any connectors to other antennas.
  • Check with multimeter the presence of 5V on the SMA socket for HF.
  • Close everything and connect the MiniWhip SDR+ antenna.

  • The MiniWhip SDR group can easily be fitted into a 20mm PVC pipe, optionally some silicone glue allows you to stop and seal the whole thing.
  • The antenna must always be positioned outside, away from metal obstacles, the PVC tube/pole can also be positioned horizontally when vertical is not possible, important at least 2 meters away from the metal part of the balcony terrace.

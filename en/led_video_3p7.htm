Video illuminator 30W Power LED

Powerful torch of over 3000 Lumens - excellent solution for diving. "DIY" by R.Chirio

The LED P7 are no longer available, it is still possible to make the illuminator with other types of LEDs, like the CREE XM-L T6 or COB LEDs of various powers.

April 2009

Simple implementation with great performance. 

There is not yet a commercial product comparable to the performance obtained with three Seoul P7 LEDs used in series at maximum power.

With this simple optical assembly it's possible to make a powerful underwater lamp for underwater video recording. Just make a waterproof aluminum container that also has space for the batteries.

Optical group.

  • The group composed of LED P7 with Fraen lenses 29°.
  • Everything mounted on an aluminum plate 80 mm diameter 3 mm thick.

January 2014

  • The group composed of 10W Warm 3000°K COB LEDs with Fraen 29° lenses.
  • Fraen lenses are cut to cover the entire emission area of 10W LEDs
  • The light emitted has a lower efficiency compared to leds at 6300°K, but the light is very pleasant and does not tire the sight, spectrum very similar to halogen lamps.
  • Each LED draws 0.9A at 10.2V
  • Series regulator circuit as indicated further on, to exploit the battery up to 10.80V

- The rear heatsink is just enough to handle the power of the three LEDs on, for continuous operation in hot environments a 12V fan is necessary.

Circuit diagram for 12V 7Ah batteries

For safety a thermal switch was placed on the LED plate, turns off if temperature exceeds 70°.

It is good to put a 4AT fuse in series with the batteries to protect against short circuits.

Circuit diagram for 12V batteries current regulation from 100 to 2800mA

With this circuit it is possible to control and stabilize the current flowing through the LEDs.

  • Via potentiometer R5 it is possible to adjust the brightness of the LEDs from nearly off to maximum, compensating for battery discharge. 
  • The Mos-Fet must be mounted on a heatsink.
  • This linear regulation configuration is particularly effective because it already starts regulating with 100 mV drop, allowing maximum exploitation of the battery charge range.

Comments

  • Simple and robust implementation, suitable for all applications where strong illumination is needed.
  • Excellent for making good quality films, outdoors at night, as well as in dark rooms or bunkers and fortifications.
  • Very valid for underwater lamps, the solution of three P7 LEDs was tested underwater and the results are exceptional, consumption of only 30W allows long runtimes, using two 12V 7A batteries you exceed 4 hours of runtime...

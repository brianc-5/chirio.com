Chirio Yagi PMR antenna 3, 5, 7 elements

Directive antenna narrow band with low swr

Radio & antennasRoberto Chiriorubric "Radio Corner"

January 2021

RTX antenna suitable for PMR frequencies from 446.000 to 446.100 MHz, certified PMR radios have fixed antenna and little can be done to connect an external antenna, but those with radio type Icom, Yaesu or Baofeng with connector output can connect an external antenna with obvious advantages to increase connection distances, which at 70cm frequencies are purely line-of-sight, that is the receiving antenna and vice versa must have optical line of sight with each other, little or no advantage is gained from reflection, as happens instead on 2m.

Yagi-type antenna, directional for 446 MHz frequencies, designed to provide maximum efficiency at PMR frequencies, available with 3 or 5 elements, or custom designs on request up to 20 elements.

The Gamma-match is adjustable to find the best SWR matching. 

Fine tuning of the dipole is also possible to center the frequency. The antenna is supplied already tuned to the center of the PMR range with the corresponding printed SWR diagram.

  • At the ends of the dipole you can see the screws for fine tuning the resonance frequency, on request the antenna can be supplied on other frequencies in the 70cm band from 430 to 450 MHz always with very low SWR.
  • You can see the gamma match very well with the screw terminal, the center is directly soldered to the BNC termination to facilitate any eventual adjustment.  

Characteristics

  • Working frequency 446.00-446.100 MHz SWR better than 1.1:1
  • Bandwidth SWR 2:1 from 445 to 447 MHz
  • Output impedance is 50 ohm BNC-F output.
  • Applicable power 20W, max 50W
  • Weight 200g (5 elements) 150g (3 elements)
  • Dimensions 70cm x 33cm (5 elements)

Chirio Yagi PMR 7 elements 9.8dB gain

- The Chirio Yagi PMR 7 element antenna can be used individually or kits are available to couple two identical antennas to increase both gain and maximum applicable power.

Tips for using the Chirio Yagi PMR

  • Antenna suitable for portable external use, it can also be fixed vertically on a photo stand, or in fixed position on a pole.
  • The Chirio Yagi PMR is very light, therefore ideal for field use, suitable therefore for signal search, such as fox hunting or for emergency search and rescue.
  • The connecting cable should be at least a good quality RG58 to minimize losses, better a cable with Teflon insulation, moreover the length of the coaxial cable must be determined and cut for the working frequency. (If needed I can provide cable cut and terminated to the right size to have the lowest possible SWR)
  • On request also model for 430 MHz Radio amateurs band.

- The robust fastening for the photo tripod, with double vertical and horizontal drilling.

LED Light Emitting Diodes.

What is an LED, how to power an LED. "DIY" by R.Chirio

LED & lightingRoberto Chirio

 LED is the acronym for Light Emitting Diode (light emitting diode). 

Introduced in the 70s as indicator lights, the first ones were red, then green and yellow.

Over time they have become important and multiple improvements have made LEDs become competitive and even exceed the efficiency of the main artificial light sources.

From the initial form with small lens, many cases suitable for all applications have then been developed...

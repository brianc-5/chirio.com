10W Power LED illuminator

Powerful portable torch of 1000 Lumen - excellent solution for work lamp with long runtime. "DIY" by Roberto Chirio

LED & lightingRoberto Chirio

This portable illuminator was built using a 12V 7Ah Dryfit battery and a 10W BK3/S spotlamp. We get over 5 hours of runtime with strong brightness or, by reducing the LED power, it's possible to have 20-30 hours of light runtime.

Everything can be easily transported thanks to the convenient handle and charge the battery with the 220V battery charger.

The structure is self-supporting and rotates entirely on the base made with the lead battery. The 12V 7Ah anti-theft type battery was chosen because it's economical and widely available. It's possible to buy for 17 euros and a cheap 12V battery charger costs the same.

It is good to put a 4AT fuse in series with the batteries to protect against short circuits.

Comments

Simple and robust design, suitable for all applications where strong lighting is needed.

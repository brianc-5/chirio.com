PARKSIDE battery pack 20V 15Ah

How to increase 20V tool runtime "DIY" by R.Chirio

Batteries & energyRoberto Chirio

March 2023

In March there were quite a few offers regarding Parkside power tools, personally I purchased several products with a 20V battery, it is immediately evident that often the battery costs more than the tool itself, so I decided to make a decidedly high-performance battery pack using scooter battery packs I had available.

Parkside is the Lidl tool line, an international discount chain. The company offers a wide range of manual and electric tools at an affordable price for consumers.
Parkside was launched by Lidl in 2010 as a line of high-quality tools at affordable prices. The product range includes hand tools such as screwdrivers, pliers and hammers, as well as power tools such as drills, grinders, saws and so on. The mission of this brand is to provide customers with reliable and durable tools that can be used to meet customers' everyday needs.

- This is a 36V 7.5Ah 270Wh scooter battery used mainly on mid-range scooters; it is characterized by an insulating plastic cage with insulating and containment functions for the 30 18650 cells rated 2.5Ah each. The BMS and its components are seen on top. The battery is completely disassembled, the BMS is removed and the cells are repositioned for a configuration of 5 groups in series composed of 6 cells in parallel.

- This is the result after soldering the cell groups, still a 270Wh battery but at 18V 15Ah, only the BMS connection is missing

  

- To connect the 21V 15Ah battery to the various tools 1m of soft cable 2x2.5mmq is used, while brass clips suitable for blade contacts have been used. The polarity must be respected as written on the original pack. The third contact, the internal one is for the temperature sensor, NTC you must mount one of 10k ohm in contact with the cells, in case of heating it shuts down the delivery to the power tool motor. If you don't want to mount the NTC just connect a 10k resistance to the nearby contact which is the negative (-); a possible thermal contact (Klixon) in series with the 10K can act as thermal protection.

Consumption tests

We will perform some practical tests to detect the consumption in Wh and understand how much work can be done with one battery charge.

The Power meter is connected in series to the power supply cable.

Suzuki tire change

Drill Parkside 400Nm

  • Battery pack 15Ah 270Wh with cable.
  • 5 bolts of 19 for each wheel.
  • Total 20 bolts unscrewed and then screwed back.
  • Drill on 200Nm position
  • Consumption 0.060Ah 1.2Wh /bolt

Total consumption 1.2Ah equal to 24Wh

  • With the 15A 270Wh pack, it is possible to change tires on over 10 vehicles.
  • With the original 2Ah 40Wh pack, it is difficult to complete a car due to the high current required (13A)
  • With the original 4Ah 80Wh pack, it is conceivable to finish 3 vehicles with 16 bolts 17mm (to be tested)
